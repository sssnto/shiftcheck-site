ShiftCheck 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 12 日

ShiftCheck 采用离线优先设计，工作与工资记录保留在你的 iPhone 上。

数据收集

ShiftCheck 不收集、传输、出售或共享个人数据。App 无需注册账号，也不包含广告、分析或跨 App 跟踪功能。

存储在设备上的数据

你输入的雇主、排班、实际班次、工资规则、工资单金额和提醒设置等信息均存储在本机。ShiftCheck 仅使用这些信息提供排班、工时记录、税前工资估算和工资核对功能。

系统功能

在你选择启用时，ShiftCheck 可以使用 iOS 提供的本地通知、小组件、实时活动、Siri 和快捷指令。这些系统功能不会使 ShiftCheck 将你的工作或工资记录发送给开发者。

导出

只有在你主动操作时，ShiftCheck 才会生成 PDF 或 CSV 报告。是否保存或分享导出文件，以及保存或分享至何处，均由你决定。

数据保留与删除

记录会保留在你的设备上，直到你在 ShiftCheck 中删除记录或移除 App。删除 App 会移除其本地数据，但仍受你与 Apple 控制的常规 iOS 备份机制影响。

儿童隐私

ShiftCheck 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，开发者也不会主动收集儿童的个人数据。

政策变更

如果 ShiftCheck 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在相关版本发布前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私披露。

联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请发送邮件至 133317737@qq.com。

ShiftCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

ShiftCheck is designed to work offline and keep your work and pay records on your iPhone.

Data Collection

ShiftCheck does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Information you enter—including employers, schedules, shifts, pay rules, pay-stub amounts, and reminder settings—is stored locally on your device. ShiftCheck uses this information only to provide its scheduling, time-tracking, gross-pay estimation, and paycheck-comparison features.

System Features

ShiftCheck may use Apple system features such as local notifications, widgets, Live Activities, Siri, and Shortcuts when you choose to enable them. These features are provided through iOS and do not cause ShiftCheck to send your work or pay records to the developer.

Exports

PDF and CSV reports are created only when you request them. You decide whether and where to save or share an exported file.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them in ShiftCheck or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior controlled by you and Apple.

Children’s Privacy

ShiftCheck is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If ShiftCheck’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the affected version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email 133317737@qq.com.
